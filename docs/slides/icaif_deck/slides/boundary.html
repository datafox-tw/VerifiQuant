Where the claim stops. It holds for single-number, formula-grounded questions inside the library with a correct contract. The silent wrongs that remain trace to the three LLM steps: selection, binding, and recalling a convention. We check that a value fits the schema and bounds, not where it came from. And we do not claim compliance. If asked about a bigger model: Gemini 2.5 Pro narrows the gap to 11.7 points, 95% CI [1.7, 21.5], mostly in ambiguity. If asked about scale: as the library grows tenfold, in-library accuracy stays between 83 and 87%, while out-of-library silent wrongs rise from 2.2 to 4.8%, not monotonically.
