VerifiQuant · ICAIF ’26

5

Five reasons a question may not justify a number, each with its own letter. N: no contract applies. M: the intent is unclear, several quantities fit. F: a required input is missing. E: a value breaks a declared check. I: a convention is left open, such as payment timing. The sixth letter, C, is the computation itself. These letters are also the gates of the funnel you will see in a moment.
