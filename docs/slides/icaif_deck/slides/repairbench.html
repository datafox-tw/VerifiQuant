To test the verifier we generated 906 faulty patches on purpose, from 179 specs and six error classes. 27 turned out to be equivalent to the original. Of the other 879 it rejects 97.7%, including every structural attack such as imports or dunder access. 20 pass. This is bounded numerical validation, not a formal proof. A wrong invariant paired with a compensating patch would also pass. Most of the 20 sit in guard branches that none of the five sampled bindings reached. Always say the denominator: 879.
