What this site shows

  • Paper replay. Recorded outputs of R-PAL, R-PAL+ and VerifiQuant on all 80 traps and three clean questions, taken from the runs behind the paper's tables and scored with the paper's scorer. The replay makes no model calls.
  • Verified repair. The loan contract's declared annuity-due transform, checked live by the same verifier the paper evaluates. It makes no model calls.
  • Try it live. The full funnel on the paper's 250-contract library with the top 4 contracts retrieved, on Gemini 2.5 Flash. Selection, binding and ambiguity detection are LLM steps, so a live result can differ from a recorded one.

What the system guarantees, and what it does not

  • Arithmetic stays outside the model. A number comes only from a contract's own code, after every gate passes.
  • Inputs are checked for schema and bounds, not for provenance. A gate cannot tell whether a bound value came from the right field.
  • Records replay as stored. Deterministic steps re-execute exactly from the stored binding. A fresh run from the raw text can take a different path.
  • Repairs are bounded numerical validation, not formal equivalence: an edit must agree with a declared invariant on sampled bindings.
  • Out of library it gives a scope signal, not more answers. Out-of-library means a formula family absent from the library, within the same benchmark.
